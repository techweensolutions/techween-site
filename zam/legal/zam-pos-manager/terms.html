1. What ZAM POS Manager is

ZAM POS Manager (the "App") is the app in which a business that accepts ZAM payments manages its tills. Its administrators use it to see the day's takings and payments, add and edit tills, link devices to tills, create keys for online checkout, set the manager PIN for voids, void payments, see balances, and manage the business's other administrators.

The App is software published by Techween. Your business's acceptance of ZAM payments, its fees and its settlement are governed by the ZAM Merchant Agreement your business signed with its bank; these Terms govern the App.

2. Who may use the App

The App is for administrators of a business that has a ZAM Merchant Agreement with a participating bank. An administrator is created by a super administrator of the business (the first one at onboarding) at one of three levels: super (everything, including other administrators), manager (tills, devices and payments) or view only (sees everything, changes nothing). Your business is responsible for whom it makes an administrator and for everything done in the App under their accounts.

4. Signing in

You sign in with your business email address. The ZAM Service sends a one-time code to that address, valid for a few minutes; you enter it in the App, which then keeps you signed in on that phone until the session expires or you sign out. There is no password: whoever can read your email can sign in as you. Protect that mailbox, use an address only you read, and do not share codes. Each administrator must have their own email address.

5. Administering tills and payments

  • Link codes. A link code turns a device into one of your tills. Send it only to the person who will link that device, and only by a channel you trust; anyone who uses it can take payments as your business.
  • Checkout keys. A key for online checkout is shown once, when it is created. Store it in your website's server configuration, never in a web page or an email, and revoke it at once if it may have been exposed.
  • Void PIN. The PIN lets anyone who knows it void an approved payment at a till on the same business day. Give it only to the managers who need it, change it when one of them leaves, and remember that the App cannot show it back to you.
  • Voids. A void returns the customer's money. Void only a payment that should not stand, and give the reason.
  • Administrators. Remove an administrator, or switch off their sign-in, as soon as they no longer need access. Switching it off ends their session within about 30 seconds.

6. Customer information you see

Payments in the App show the name each customer's bank supplies for receipts (the "display name"), authorization codes and payment references. Use them only to run, reconcile and support your sales. Do not copy them into other systems or use them for marketing. When you copy or share a payment from the App, you are responsible for where it goes.
