ZAM POS Manager

Account and data deletion

Request deletion of your administrator account

In the app, open More and choose Request account deletion.

Techween support handles the request within 30 days. If you cannot sign in, write to support@techween.net from the email address of your administrator account.

What is deleted and what is not

  • Your administrator record is removed. Until then, your business's super administrators can switch off your access at any time.
  • Records about your business, its tills and its payments are held for your business's bank: ask the bank about them.
  • Records that Central Bank of Kuwait rules require to be kept are kept even after a deletion request: payment records, audit logs and request logs are kept for at least ten (10) years. Records of what you did as an administrator are part of the audit records.

On your phone

Signing out removes your session and administrator record from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes the rest.
