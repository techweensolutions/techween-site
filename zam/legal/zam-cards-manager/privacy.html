1. Who is responsible for your data

ZAM Cards Manager (the "App") is published by Techween Integrated Computer Solutions Company ("Techween") for the banks that offer it. In this Notice, "the Bank" is the bank you choose in the App: the bank that issues your ZAM cards.

The Bank is the controller of all personal data processed in the App. Techween is the Bank's processor: it publishes the App, hosts and operates the systems behind it, and processes your data only on the Bank's instructions, under a written data processing agreement, and may not use it for any purpose of its own. Your data is information about a customer of the Bank and is protected by Kuwaiti banking secrecy law, which binds Techween and its staff as it binds the Bank. Each bank's data is kept apart from every other bank's.

This Notice explains what the App and the systems behind it do with your data. The Bank's own privacy notice explains what else the Bank does with it.

The App does not ask for your civil ID, a password or your banking credentials. It does not process your account balances or statements, your location, your contacts, your photos, biometric data or any advertising identifier, and it contains no advertising software and no analytics or crash reporting by Techween or the Bank.

The only phone permissions the App asks for are notifications and the camera. The camera is used to scan the QR of a link code; the picture is read on the phone and is neither stored nor sent anywhere. On Android the scanner is Google's ML Kit library, which sends Google diagnostic information about its own working (the phone's model and system build, the App's version, performance figures and error codes, with an identifier for this installation of the App), and never the picture or the code.

3. Why the data is processed and on what basis

To provide the App and your ZAM cards under the Bank's card terms and the App's Terms of Use; to protect you and the Bank against fraud and unauthorised use; and to meet the Bank's legal obligations under Central Bank of Kuwait instructions, the anti-money-laundering law and record-keeping rules.

4. Who receives your data

  • Techween, as the Bank's processor, to host and operate the systems named above.
  • Techween's sub-processors, approved by the Bank and bound by contract: cloud hosting for the Bank's ZAM Issuer instance (Google Cloud; Section 5 says where) and push notification delivery (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which receives only a device token and the notification text). On Android, Google also receives the scanner's diagnostic information described in Section 2. Your name, civil ID and contact details do not leave the Bank.
  • The merchants you pay receive, through the ZAM Cards app, only the amount, the time, a payment reference and a display name; never your card details. The App itself shares nothing with merchants.
  • Regulators and authorities where the laws of Kuwait require.

The Bank does not sell your data and does not share it with any other interactive service provider or third party without your explicit, prior consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Techween does not sell your data or use it for any purpose of its own.

5. Where your data is kept

The ZAM Connector runs inside the Bank's own premises in Kuwait. With the Central Bank of Kuwait's no-objection, the Bank's ZAM Issuer instance is hosted by Techween for the Bank in Google Cloud's Doha, Qatar region; if the Central Bank of Kuwait requires the data to stay in Kuwait, it is hosted in Kuwait. Either way it is encrypted, in an environment dedicated to the Bank, with access limited to named, screened Techween staff in Kuwait under the Bank's audit.

7. How it is protected

Every connection uses TLS 1.2 or higher. The App never asks for your civil ID or your banking credentials. A link code works once and only for a short time, and a phone can manage your cards only after you activate it in the Bank's own channel; you or the Bank can revoke it at any time. Card-to-account links are held only inside the Bank. Cards pay only from registered phones, each confirmed by you and re-confirmed yearly. Three wrong card link codes lock a phone out for 15 minutes. The App does not run on a rooted, jailbroken or modified phone. Access to the systems behind the App by Techween staff is limited, individually attributed and logged. The Bank and its regulator may audit Techween at any time.

8. Your rights

You may ask the Bank to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw any consent you have given. Contact the Bank through its customer service channels.

You can remove what the App's service holds about you yourself: in the App, open Profile and choose Delete my account. This erases your profile, sessions, push token and account nicknames from the ZAM Connector and unlinks your phones from ZAM Cards Manager. It does not close your accounts or cancel your cards, which stay with the Bank. Records the Bank must keep by law are kept even after a deletion request.

For anything Techween does as the Bank's processor you may also write to Techween at the address in Section 10; Techween passes requests about your data to the Bank. You may complain to the Central Bank of Kuwait or to the Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority (CITRA) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

9. Changes to this Notice

Changes to this Notice are published in the App and on techween.net, and you are notified before material changes take effect; the App asks you to acknowledge the new version before you continue. The version and effective date are shown at the top of this Notice.
