ZAM Cards Manager

Account and data deletion

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete my account.

This removes the data the ZAM Connector holds for your use of ZAM Cards Manager. It does not close your bank accounts or cancel your cards: they stay with your bank, which you can ask to do either.

What is not deleted

  • Data your bank holds: ask your bank to access, correct or delete it.
  • Records that Central Bank of Kuwait rules require to be kept are kept even after a deletion request: payment records, audit logs and request logs are kept for at least ten (10) years.

If you cannot open the app

Write to support@techween.net from the email address you use with the bank, and we will pass your request to the bank that offers the app.
