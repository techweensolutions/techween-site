ZAM POS

Account and data deletion

There are no personal accounts in ZAM POS

ZAM POS does not ask you to create an account, so there is no personal account to delete. The app is linked to a till of your business, not to a person.

Delete what the app holds on the device

  • In ZAM POS, open Settings and tap Unlink Device; or have an administrator of your business unlink the till from ZAM POS Manager; or remove the app. Each erases the till's credential, settings and paired display from the device.
  • Delete any statements you exported; they stay on the device until you delete them.

What is not deleted

  • The till's device registration at the ZAM Service stays, with the payment records, once the till is unlinked.
  • Records that Central Bank of Kuwait rules require to be kept are kept even after a deletion request: payment records, audit logs and request logs are kept for at least ten (10) years.
  • Records about your business and its payments are held for your business's bank: ask the bank about them.
