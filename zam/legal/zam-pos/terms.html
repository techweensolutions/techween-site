1. What ZAM POS is

ZAM POS (the "App") is the till app of the ZAM payment service. A merchant's staff use it to ask a customer for a payment, show the customer a QR code (and, where switched on, a payment sound) that the customer's ZAM Cards app picks up, and see the result within seconds. The App also shows the till's payment history and statements, can send each payment to a customer display (the ZAM Screen app), and can cancel a request or void a payment where the merchant allows it.

4. Linking a till

  1. An administrator of your business creates a link code for one of your tills in the ZAM POS Manager app or the merchant portal. A link code is single-use and expires after the time the administrator chooses.
  2. You type the code, scan it as a QR code, or open the link the administrator sends you. The App then registers the device with the ZAM Service as that till and receives a credential that it keeps in the device's secure storage.
  3. From then on the device acts as that till until an administrator unlinks it, or you choose Unlink device in Settings.

Do not link a device you do not control, and never share a link code: whoever uses it gets a till in your business's name.

5. Taking payments

  • You enter the amount and, if you wish, a note, and the App creates a payment request. The customer pays by scanning the QR code with ZAM Cards or, where pay by sound is on, by letting their phone hear the till. The App shows Approved or Declined as soon as the customer's bank answers.
  • A request is valid for a limited time (15 minutes at a till by default) and can be approved only once.
  • Check the amount before you show the code. A customer approves exactly the amount you entered.
  • Hand over goods or services only after the App shows the payment as approved.
  • You can cancel a request that has not been paid. Where your business has set a void PIN, a manager can void an approved payment on the same business day by entering the PIN; after repeated wrong PINs voids are locked for 15 minutes. After the business day, or for part of the amount, a payment can be refunded within 180 days through the merchant portal, under the Merchant Agreement.

6. Customer information you see

For each approved payment the App shows the name the customer's bank supplies for receipts (the "display name"), the authorization code and the payment reference. Use them only to complete, record and support that sale. Do not copy them into other systems, show them to other customers, or use them for marketing. Statements you export from the App contain the same information and are your business's records to protect (see Part B, Section 2.4).

7. The customer display

You can pair the App with a ZAM Screen on the same local network by entering the code the Screen shows. The App then sends the Screen what the customer needs to see: the amount, the QR code, the note and, after payment, the result and the customer's display name. The connection between the till and the Screen on your local network is not encrypted. Pair them only on a private network your business controls, never on a public or guest Wi-Fi.
