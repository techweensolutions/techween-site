1. What ZAM Screen is

ZAM Screen (the "App") turns a tablet, phone or computer into a display that faces your customers at the till. Paired with a ZAM POS till on the same local network, it shows the customer the amount to pay and the payment QR code, plays the payment's sound beacon, and shows the result once the customer's bank has approved the payment.

The App is software published by Techween for businesses that accept ZAM payments under a ZAM Merchant Agreement with a participating bank. It holds no account and takes no payment itself: the till does that.

3. Pairing

The App shows a six-digit code, renewed every five minutes, and its address on your local network. A member of staff enters the code in ZAM POS (Settings → Connect a display for customers). The App then accepts instructions only from that till, until staff tap Disconnect on the App or disconnect it from the till.

4. Where to put it and how to connect it

  • Place the screen so that the paying customer can see it and scan its code, and other customers cannot read it from a distance. After a payment it shows the customer's display name, supplied by their bank, for about ten seconds.
  • Connect the App and the till only on a private network your business controls. Their connection on your local network is not encrypted, and the App announces itself on that network so that the till can find it. Never use a public or guest Wi-Fi.
  • Do not leave a paired screen where the public can handle it. Disconnect it before moving it, lending it, repairing it or giving it away.
