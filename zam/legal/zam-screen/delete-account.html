ZAM Screen

Account and data deletion

There are no accounts in ZAM Screen

ZAM Screen does not ask you to create an account, and it sends nothing to Techween, so Techween holds no data about you from this app and there is nothing to delete here.

Delete what the app holds on the device

Disconnect the app from the till in the app: this erases the pairing token and the business and till details from the device. Uninstalling the app removes the rest. Payment details shown on screen are never written to the device.
