Support

Contact us

Write to support@techween.net. Techween answers questions about how the app works and about problems with the app itself.

What to tell us

  • The name of the app and its version
  • The model of your phone or device and its operating system version
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened; a screenshot helps

Please do not send passwords, one-time codes, PINs, full card numbers or your civil ID number by email.

Who can help with what

  • The app will not open, a card will not link, or the app behaves unexpectedly: write to us.
  • Your card, its limits, a payment you did not make, or a refund: contact your issuing bank. For a refund, ask the merchant; the refund is credited to the same ZAM card by your bank.

Complaints

You may also complain to the Central Bank of Kuwait or to the Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority (CITRA).
