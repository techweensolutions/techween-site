ZAM Cards

Account and data deletion

There is no ZAM Cards account

ZAM Cards does not ask you to create an account with Techween. Your card, identity and account data are held by your issuing bank. The app holds only what is needed to pay with a card on your phone.

Delete what the app holds

  1. Open ZAM Cards and choose the card.
  2. Tap Unlink Card and confirm.

Unlinking removes the card's credentials and keys from your phone and asks your bank to unlink the phone from the card. To remove everything else the app keeps on the phone, such as settings, uninstall the app.

What is not deleted by unlinking

  • Data your bank holds (card, identity, account and transaction data): ask your issuing bank to access, correct or delete it.
  • Records that Central Bank of Kuwait rules require to be kept are kept even after a deletion request: payment records, audit logs and request logs are kept for at least ten (10) years.
