1. What Salfa is

Salfa (the "App") is a free messaging app for iPhone and Android. You can send messages, photos, videos, documents and voice notes, chat in groups of up to 50 people, and make voice and video calls. Everything you send is end-to-end encrypted: it is locked on your phone and can be unlocked only on the phones of the people you send it to. Techween runs the servers that pass messages along, but cannot read them.

Once a message reaches the other person's phone, it lives only on the phones in the chat. Salfa keeps no copy of your history on its servers.

The App records on your phone which version you accepted and when. If we change these Terms in a way that matters, the App asks you again before you can keep using it. You can read both documents at any time in the App, under Settings, and on techween.net.

4. Your account

  • Email and code. Your account is your email address. We send a one-time code to it when you sign up and when you sign in on a new phone.
  • PIN. You choose a PIN of at least six digits. It is needed, with the email code, to put your account on a new phone. Keep it secret; Techween never asks for it outside the App.
  • One phone. An account works on one phone at a time. Signing in on a new phone switches the old one off.
  • Backup email. You may add a backup email address in Settings. It is the only way to recover your account if you forget your PIN or lose your main mailbox, and some recoveries wait 7 days so that you can stop one you did not ask for. Without a backup email, a forgotten PIN or a lost mailbox cannot be recovered by anyone, Techween included.
  • You are responsible for what happens through your account, and for keeping your phone, your mailbox and your PIN safe.

5. Your messages are kept on your phone, and you keep them

  • Your chat history, photos and files are stored only on your phone, encrypted, in the place you choose: inside the App, or a folder or memory card your phone allows.
  • Techween cannot restore your history. If you lose your phone, delete the App, erase your phone or remove the memory card, your history is gone unless you made a backup.
  • The App can make an encrypted backup file, protected by a passphrase you choose, and save it where you pick. Keeping that file and remembering its passphrase is up to you: a forgotten passphrase cannot be recovered.
  • A message waits on our servers, encrypted, only until the other person's phone collects it. If it is not collected within 30 days it is deleted, and your phone shows it as not delivered.
  • Disappearing messages and "delete for everyone" depend on the other person's phone running the App as published. They cannot remove a copy someone has already saved, forwarded or photographed.

7. Chat requests, blocking, reporting and suspension

  • Nobody can message you until you agree. A stranger can send you only a chat request showing their name and verified email, with no message text. Nothing else from them reaches you until you accept.
  • Block anyone from their chat or profile: nothing more from them reaches you, and they are not told.
  • Report anyone from their chat or profile. A report goes to Techween's staff with the reason you choose and, only if you choose, a note and the last messages of that chat. The App shows you exactly what will be sent before you send it. Those messages leave the encryption as plain text, because staff need to read them.
  • Techween's staff look at reports within 24 hours. Because we cannot read chats, we act on what reports contain and on patterns of reports. We may suspend an account that breaks these Terms, without notice where the harm justifies it. A suspended account can still read its history on the phone but cannot send or receive. Where the law requires it, we may report illegal content to the authorities.
  • If you think your account was suspended by mistake, write to support@techween.net from the account's email address.

8. Calls

Calls are encrypted between the two phones. Where the phones cannot connect directly, the call passes through Techween's relay servers, which cannot open it. When phones connect directly, each can see the other's internet address; turn on Always relay calls in Settings to hide yours. Salfa is not a telephone service and cannot call emergency numbers. Use your phone's own dialler for emergencies.
