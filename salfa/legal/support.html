Salfa: terms and privacy

Salfa

Support

Issued by Techween Integrated Computer Solutions Company

Contact us

Write to support@techween.net, from the email address of your Salfa account if your question is about your account.

What to tell us

  • Your phone's model and its iOS or Android version, and the Salfa version (Settings → About)
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened; a screenshot helps

Never send your PIN, a sign-in code or your backup passphrase, to us or to anyone. Techween will never ask for them.

Common questions

  • I forgot my PIN, or lost access to my email. In the App, choose Forgot PIN? at the PIN step. You need the backup email you added in Settings; some recoveries wait 7 days for your safety. Without a backup email, nobody can recover the account.
  • I lost my phone or reinstalled the App. Sign in again with your email and PIN, and restore your latest backup file if you have one. Techween cannot restore your history: it was only on your phone.
  • Someone is bothering me. Open their chat or profile, choose Block, and Report them if they break the rules.
  • I do not get notifications. Check that notifications are allowed for Salfa in your phone's settings, and that battery saving does not stop it.

Report abuse

Use Report in the App: it lets you include the messages we need to see. You can also write to support@techween.net. Staff look at reports within 24 hours.
