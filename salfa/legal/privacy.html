Privacy Policy

Issued by Techween Integrated Computer Solutions Company
Version 1.0

Draft — not yet in force

1. In short

  • We cannot read your messages, files or calls. They are end-to-end encrypted on your phone. Techween never has the keys.
  • We keep nothing after delivery. An encrypted message waits on our server only until the other phone collects it, at most 30 days. Your history lives only on your phone.
  • We keep what an account needs: your email address, a check value for your PIN, your phone's public keys and its notification address, and the lists that make delivery work.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data. The App contains no advertising or analytics code and does not upload your address book.

2. Who is responsible

Techween Integrated Computer Solutions Company, of the State of Kuwait, develops and operates Salfa and is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: support@techween.net · Unit 45, Reem Marzouq Mohammed Al-Ghanim Building, Abdulaziz Hamad Al-Saqer Street, Block 2, Al-Mirqab, Capital Governorate, State of Kuwait.

3. What we can never see

The content of your messages, photos, videos, documents and voice notes; your calls; your display name and profile photo; the names and photos of your groups; the list of people you chat with one to one; your chat history. All of these are encrypted on your phone with keys that only the phones in the chat hold, using the published MLS standard (RFC 9420) for messages and DTLS-SRTP for calls.

5. What passes through without being recorded

While the service works, our servers necessarily see who is sending to whom and when, your internet address, and whether your App is connected. We do not record these, except as listed above. Our call relays forward encrypted call packets and keep no record of calls.

6. What other people can see

  • People who know your email address can find out that it has a Salfa account, and send you a chat request.
  • People you send a chat request to see the name you give it and your verified email address.
  • Your accepted contacts receive your display name and profile photo, encrypted, and see when you are typing and when you have read their messages.
  • Members of a group see who else is in it.
  • In a call, the other phone can see your internet address when the phones connect directly, unless you turn on Always relay calls.
  • Anyone you report is not told who reported them.

7. On your phone

  • Your history and files are kept in an encrypted store whose key is held by your phone's secure hardware and cannot be copied to another device. Your encryption keys never leave the App.
  • Photos and videos you send are re-encoded and stripped of their metadata, including location, before they are encrypted.
  • The App asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it: the camera (calls, scanning a contact's security code, taking a photo to send), the microphone (calls and voice notes), photos you choose to send or use as your profile photo, Face ID or fingerprint (app lock), and notifications. It does not use your location, read your contacts, use an advertising identifier, or include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.
  • Backups are files you create and keep, encrypted with your passphrase. We never receive them.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and do not share it for advertising. These providers process data on our behalf, only to run Salfa:

9. Security

Encryption on the phone; HTTPS for every connection to our servers; PINs kept only as a slow, salted check value; servers in a private network with access limited to the staff who need it; staff actions in the moderation tool recorded by name. No system is perfectly secure: keep your phone locked and up to date, and compare security codes with the people you chat with.

10. Your rights

Under the laws of the State of Kuwait, including the Data Privacy Protection Regulation of the Communication and Information Technology Regulatory Authority (CITRA), you may ask to know what personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, and to object to how we use it. You can change your backup email in Settings, and delete your account in the App under Settings → Delete account; the page at techween.net/salfa/legal/delete-account.html explains what that removes. For anything else write to support@techween.net from your account's email address. You may also complain to CITRA.

11. Children

Salfa is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to support@techween.net and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this Policy

We will publish changes in the App and on techween.net, and ask you again in the App before a change that matters takes effect.
