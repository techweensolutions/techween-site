Salfa: terms and privacy

Salfa

Delete your account

Issued by Techween Integrated Computer Solutions Company

Delete your account in the App

  1. Open Salfa and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Read what will be removed, then enter your PIN to confirm.

The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your email addresses, your PIN check value, your phone's keys and notification address, your contact permits and block list.
  • Your place in every group. If you were the last admin, the longest-standing member becomes admin.
  • Messages and files waiting for you that had not reached your phone.
  • People can no longer find you or write to you on Salfa.

What stays

  • Your history on your phone stays until you remove it. Uninstall the App to erase it from the phone; delete any backup files you made yourself.
  • Messages you already sent stay on the phones of the people who received them. Techween cannot reach them.
  • Records of reports: if you sent a report, it no longer names you. If you were reported, the record of who reported, why and what staff decided is kept, without any message text, and no longer names you once your account is gone.
  • Technical logs, which never name an account, are deleted after 7 days.

If you cannot open the App

Write to support@techween.net from the email address of your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm the request by email and delete the account within 30 days.
